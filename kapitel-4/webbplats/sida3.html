Deco*27

Deco*27 (stylized in all caps; pronounced Deko Niina), is a Japanese musician, Vocaloid producer, and songwriter. He is considered to be one of the most influential Vocaloid producers of the early 2010s, along with Wowaka and Kenshi Yonezu (Hachi).
